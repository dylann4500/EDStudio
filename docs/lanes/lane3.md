# Lane 3: good-ending pet sims — @ed._.sims

**Decision (lane scout, Oct 26, from TikTok data below):** one recurring toy hero, **Pip the corgi puppy**. Every
episode is a what-if, a disaster or a "first" that **always ends well**. It's a series: the end card asks viewers what
Pip should do next, and their answers become the next episodes. This is the only lane with a recurring character. Lane 1 is
"everyone on Earth" chaos (`unhinged.md`) and lane 2 is physics (`science.md`); both are one-off premises with no cast.
Biographies are out: Lincoln got 307 views on this account and Musk got 85.

## What a video is
- **Pip:** a corgi puppy, orange with a white blaze, chest and paws. Big ears that perk up and droop, a fluffy tail that wags,
  big glossy eyes, a red collar with a gold "PIP" tag. He is honest, brave, a bit clumsy, and never in on the danger.
  His toy is the plush bunny (`makeBunny`, from 001) and it comes back in every episode.
- **His family** is seen from dog height: Mia (about 8, a scaled kid rig) plus Mom and Dad, mostly knees and hands until they crouch.
  Background toy people and other dogs fill the places.
- **Three episode types; rotate them:**
  - **Firsts:** first night home, first snow, first trick-or-treat, first swim. @whatif.goodending's best tier is 434K–1M.
  - **Disasters that go right:** blackout, storm, flood, tornado. The threat is real and Pip's innocence turns it.
    Their tsunami and meteor episodes got 0.9–1.3M.
  - **A day on the job / a big place:** supermarket, ring bearer, airport, school. This is Hugo's template: first job
    932K, desk job 458K. Every place is a set we already have.
- **Never:**
  - Pip is never hurt and never dies, and the ending is never sad. "It goes right" is the brand promise.
  - No IP crossovers ("Crumb in Avengers" got 28K).
  - No gimmick episodes ("evil twin" 64–100K, "zombie apocalypse" 85K).
  - Don't copy their beach, their golden retrievers or their titles.
- **Words:** a warm storybook narrator in short third-person lines ("He waited by the door."). The jokes come from Pip's
  logic ("He thought the vacuum was a monster. He was right."). No speech and no voiceover.
  Add one real, sourced pet fact per episode when it fits (AKC, ASPCA, papers), cited in `shots.js` + caption.md.

## Structure + timing (55–65 s)
| t | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–3.3 s | The question, alone and centered, over a cute close-up of Pip (this frame is the thumbnail). Phrase it as "What if…?", e.g. "What if nobody came to Pip's birthday?" |
| 3.3–9 s | Baseline: who and where. The HUD counter appears at its starting value. Quiet room tone and paws; the music enters on the premise. |
| 9–36 s | 5–6 beats of ~5 s. The problem grows and the counter moves against him. Pip tries three times (rule of three). Low point at ~60 % ("He thought everyone forgot."). |
| 36–50 s | The turn ("…or did they?"), then the good ending. The counter flips, the music lifts to a major key, and something physically big happens (a wave you can surf, every light coming back on, the whole park doing zoomies). |
| 50–56 s | Warm button: tail wagging at full speed, a lick, the family pile-on, Pip asleep with the bunny. |
| end card ≥ 4 s | Next-episode vote: "What should Pip try next? 👇" / "Where should Pip go next? 👇" / "Say happy birthday, Pip 👇". |

- **Camera = pet's-eye:** the camera sits 0.35–0.5 m off the ground and humans are seen from the knees up. Use human eye level
  only for the wide establishing shot and the last shot. Show at least 3 close-ups of Pip's face reacting (head tilt,
  ears droop, ears perk). Cut before anything goes static (house rule: ~5 s beats, motion in every frame).
- **Places:** 3–4 per episode, all inside Pip's world (home base, his street, and the episode's destination). That keeps the
  house variety rule and still fits 2 a day.
- **Continuity:** each caption starts with "Pip, ep. N:". Episodes may call back to earlier ones (the bunny, the neighbor,
  the porch). Never put an episode tag in the video itself.

## HUD
One counter that *is* the plot, small, top-left, steady (house rules): PARTY GUESTS 0 → 6 · CANDY 0 → 41 ·
HOMES WITH POWER 12,400 → 0 → 12,400 · WATER LEVEL 2 in → 3 ft → 0 · DAYS WAITED 0 → 98 · RINGS 2 → 0 → 2.
The sub-line holds the time + place ("8:14 PM · MAPLE ST"). The counter's final value lands on the good-ending beat.

## Sound
Its own identity, **cozy**: music box/`bell` + soft piano over a warm pad.
- Disaster episodes add a low drone + soft taiko under the threat, and it resolves into a major key on the turn.
- Foley: paws (soft, high-pitched `footstep`), `plush_squeak` (the bunny), `dog_bark` (soft boof), `snore`, `room_tone`,
  `crickets`, `rain_window`, `wind`, and `applause`/`crowd_cheer` for the payoff.
- Quiet open. The music never drops out. Everything low-passed. No whines or yelps louder than the bed.

## Caption
- Hook + emoji ("Pip, ep. 3: nobody came to his birthday… 🥺🐶").
- 2–3 short story lines that end on a curiosity gap ("…or did they? 👀").
- A question CTA that picks the next episode 👇.
- "Send this to your dog person".
- Blank line, the course line, blank line.
- 5 hashtags: `#animation #whatif #dogsoftiktok #corgi #<topic>`.

   - Corgi proportions: puppy head, upright ears, stubby legs, fluffy tail.

   - A collar tag and hat/costume anchors on the head.

2. **Home base set `livingroom.js`** (new): front door with a window (and the porch outside it), couch, rug, dog bed, TV,
   family photos, a window that changes season. Pip's world uses it in every episode.

   - A soft whimper SFX (low-passed).
