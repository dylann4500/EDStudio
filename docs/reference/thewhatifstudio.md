## What works
- **"Everyone on Earth at the same time"** (clap, jump, breathe: 1.5–2.2M) and **"X disappeared for 5 seconds"**
  (0.4–2.1M). These are exactly our lane 1 and lane 2, and they confirm those lanes. Lane 3 must not duplicate them.
- **"You asked for it (4,142 of you)"**: comment-requested sequels.
- Survival POV ("don't drive into town") is a newer branch at ~250–290K.

## Take for EDStudio
- These are big one-off hits, but they convert poorly to followers (0.31 follows per 100 views vs 1.05 for the recurring
  dog account), with 6 % likes and 0.45 % shares on the 2.2M hit.
- Feed the "everyone at the same time" ideas to lane 1 and the "X disappeared for 5 s" ideas to lane 2.
