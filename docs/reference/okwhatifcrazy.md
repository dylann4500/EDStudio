## What works
- **A recurring animal hero in a human job** is a repeatable template ("Hugo's first day in…"). Every job is a new set, and the
  gag is a big body in a small world (broken chair, lift, moped).
- **The audience picks the next episode** ("Best answer gets Episode 3").
- The first Hugo episode beat everything since the pinned landmark hits. The series median is ~80K: it decays without
  a heart beat, because Hugo only breaks things.
- One-off new animals (raccoons, pigeons) without a hook flopped at 8–9K.

## Take for EDStudio
Steal the **job/place template** for lane 3: a hero visiting our existing sets (supermarket, wedding, pool, plane).
Add what Hugo lacks, a warm good ending, which @whatif.goodending shows holds a ~275K median.
