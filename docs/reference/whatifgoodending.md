## Why the winners win
1. **A cute hero who doesn't know the danger.** The crowd knows the sea pulled back and the dog doesn't. That gap is the suspense,
   and the good ending pays it off: the dog rescues a starfish, and the "tsunami" was the sea breathing out.
2. **Firsts and loneliness turned into a surprise.** "It's my puppy's 1st birthday… but nobody came." The HUD reads
   PARTY GUESTS 0 over an empty living room. Then "…or did they? 👀", and everyone jumps out. That's 1M views from one set.
3. **The audience writes the series.** "You asked…", "You said…", "You voted. You named him." Each end card asks for the next
   episode ("What disaster should go right next?"), so the comments turn into the backlog.
4. **The puppy is the upgrade.** After Crumb arrived (Oct 16), 6 of 10 posts got ≥ 434K (not counting today's).
   Over Oct 11–16 the adult dog's posts had slid to 77–367K.
5. **The flops are gimmicks and borrowed IP:** evil twin 64–100K, zombies 85K, Avengers 28K.

## Rules to copy
- The hero never knows the danger, the ending is always good, and the counter *is* the plot.
- The end card asks for the next episode, and captions answer the comments ("You asked for it").
- Use the toy-scale world at the hero's eye level. Firsts and disasters alternate.

## What not to copy
- Their dogs (golden retrievers Biscuit, Cookie, Crumb), their beach town, their titles, their merch/DM-for-custom hustle.
- "Double tap to save him" engagement bait more than once per episode.
- IP crossovers and gimmick episodes.
