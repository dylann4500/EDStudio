Studied Nov 8 2026 (manager session "Scribe promo video animation styles"): logged-out reel pages in the built-in browser
(frame grids via in-page `<video>` seeks, see docs/PROMO.md), view counts from the logged-in reels tab.

**What they sell:** Write It (write-it.ai), a Chrome extension that types AI text into Google Docs keystroke by keystroke with
typos + pauses ("Typed like a human": auto typer, humanizer, AI-detector check). Their whole feed is ads for it.

**The account:** first posts Oct 20 2026; 7.7K followers on Nov 8; **5–9 reels a day** (nine on Nov 8 alone, roughly hourly).
Reach is a volume game: most reels sit at 2K–80K views, and a few break out.

**The top three are all the same format: an object or room upgraded step by step into something absurd** (our build genre,
docs/BUILD.md, is this format). Each upgrade gets a word or two of narration, the camera holds one place, the build grows.

## The structure (every reel, 55–70 s)
1. 0 s: a trend premise spoken over the first frame (upgrade X every day, stay in bed N days, everyone in my town has a counter).
2. Escalation: 6–10 upgrades / days, a counter or label on screen, one new thing every 3–4 s.
3. **~60–70 % in: the obstacle is an essay.** A laptop with a Google Doc, an "ESSAY DUE MIDNIGHT" card, an AI-detector gate
   (GPTZero / Turnitin boards that go red → green).
4. The product appears as the step that clears the obstacle: the Write It panel on the laptop typing the essay, "0 % AI".
5. Payoff: the build's biggest upgrade, "Congratulations", then "Write It … on Chrome" + the house/campus wide as the last frame.

Narration: a fast male narrator, **one word at a time in the center** (white, black outline: our build captions). No music bed
to speak of; voice-led. Captions are short ("Write it or write it or write it or join the discord pls"); the comment section is
seeded by the account ("Share this to someone who…").

## Styles they rotate
- **Sims-like realistic 3D** (low-detail realistic people, houses with a cut-away ground slab, workshop/bedroom sets) = the hits.
- **Flat 2D cartoon** (thick black outlines, big heads: "Ethan, 16", "INVENTIONS: 41 WORDS", 1982 typing): mid (10–40K).
- **Voxel / blocky** (town where everyone has a number above their head, black hole what-if): low (2–3K).
- Graphic/meme cards ("NO BOOKS NO NOTES NO CLUE", "ESSAY DUE MIDNIGHT BONK").

## What this means for Scribe
- Scribe's obstacle is not an AI detector, it's **"handwritten only"**: a worksheet, a lab notebook, show-your-work, a
  teacher who checks the handwriting. Same slot in the structure (60–70 % in), our product clears it (photo → solved → written
  in YOUR handwriting, docs/PROMO.md for the real claims and brand assets).
- Lead with the upgrade-escalation format (build harness, `/new-build`, 039's pace) and test other styles in volume.
- Post many a day; a 2M hit came from an account two weeks old.
