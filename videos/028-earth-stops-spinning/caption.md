What if Earth stopped spinning for one second? 🌍⏸️

The ground under the equator moves east at 1,674 km/h. If it stopped dead for one second, everything not bolted down would keep that speed: you'd slide 465 meters (still on the equator), pools would empty into the deep end, and the Atlantic would pull 337 meters away from Maine. At the South Pole, the ground moves at 0 km/h. Nothing happens at all.
