What if the Moon were twice as close? 🌕🌊

It would look twice as wide and shine four times as bright. Its pull on the oceans grows 8×, so a 1.5-meter harbor tide becomes a 12-meter one, and a total eclipse lasts about half an hour instead of 7½ minutes. A "month" from full moon to full moon shrinks to 10 days, which means 37 full moons a year.
