What if the oxygen in the air doubled overnight? 🫁🔥

Air is 21 % oxygen. At 42 %, breathing feels amazing for a while: runners find a new gear, tired lungs breathe easy. But fire feeds on oxygen too: candles roar, barbecues turn into flamethrowers, and safety codes already call anything above 23.5 % "oxygen-enriched". Give it millions of years and you get dragonflies with 70 cm wings, like the ones that flew 300 million years ago.

Would you breathe double the oxygen? 👇
