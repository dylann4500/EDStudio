How a Flashlight Becomes a Lighthouse 🔦🌊

Ships keep bonking Gary's rock, so he waves a flashlight at them, and a seagull steals the batteries. Then come a tower tall enough to see over the curve of the Earth, a copper mirror, a two-ton Fresnel lens floating on mercury, clockwork weights, a foghorn and a black-and-white spiral. Every fix is real lighthouse engineering, and every one of them happens to Gary first.
