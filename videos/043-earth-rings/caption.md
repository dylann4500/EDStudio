What if Earth had rings like Saturn? 🪐🌍

Shrink Saturn's rings to fit Earth and they'd sit 1,500 to 8,000 km up, too close for the ice to ever clump into a moon. From the equator they're a thin line straight overhead; from New York or Tokyo they're a giant arch, and at night they glow brighter than any full Moon. But they cast a shadow too: in December, Miami's noon sun would hide behind them. Scientists even propose Earth had a ring 466 million years ago.
