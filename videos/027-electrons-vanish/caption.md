What if every electron disappeared? ⚛️💥

Every light, every sky, every thought stops at once, because they all run on electrons. What's left is bare nuclei, all positive, pushing each other apart a trillion trillion trillion times harder than gravity pulls them together. In 21 milliseconds, Earth is a cloud twice its size.

Which would you rather lose: gravity or electrons? 👇
