How a Hair Dryer Becomes a Jet Engine ✈️💨

Gary wants to fly, so he tapes a hair dryer to a cardboard plane, and it pushes about as hard as an egg weighs. Then come a fan in a tube, six compressor stages, a flame hotter than lava, a turbine on the same shaft, a nozzle, blades with cooling holes and a giant fan up front. Every fix is real jet engine engineering, and every one of them happens to Gary first.
