What if the Sun disappeared? ☀️➡️🌑

You wouldn't know for 8 minutes and 19 seconds. That's how long sunlight takes to reach us, and the Sun's gravity travels at the same speed, so Earth keeps orbiting nothing while everyone has a normal day. Then it goes dark everywhere at once, Earth flies off in a straight line at 30 km/s, and scientists estimate the average temperature hits −18 °C within a week.
