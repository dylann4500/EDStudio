What if gravity turned off for 5 seconds? 🌍⬆️

You wouldn't just float away, but anything already moving up would keep going. Every fan mid-jump rises 14 meters, a diver turns a 3-meter board into a 27-meter cliff dive, and the ocean, squeezed by its own weight, springs up about 35 meters. Then gravity comes back, everywhere at once.
