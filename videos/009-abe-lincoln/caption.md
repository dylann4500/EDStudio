# Caption

From a dirt-floor log cabin to the Lincoln Memorial 🎩🪓

Less than one year of school. An axe in his hands from age 7. He read by firelight, walked miles to return a few cents, taught himself law — and ended up holding a breaking country together and signing the Emancipation Proclamation. 56 years, one unbelievable life.
